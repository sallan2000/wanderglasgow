---
name: Access claims
description: Preserve the distinction between attraction access information and route accessibility.
---

Owner-managed attraction access information must remain separate from claims about walking-route accessibility. Missing information is Unknown, not Yes or No; only an explicit suitable value qualifies for a requested access filter.

**Why:** The product scope requires owner-entered access details without automatic venue certification, and explicitly forbids implying that the connecting route is accessible. The walking provider supplies routing distances, not an accessibility assessment.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when adding further filters, curated-walk details or email content. Do not infer an accessible route from suitable attraction entrances or replace unknown information with positive claims.
