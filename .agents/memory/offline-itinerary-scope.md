---
name: Offline itinerary scope
description: Keep explicit local itinerary exports distinct from server-resolved emails and whole-site offline support.
---

Offline itinerary copies are explicit local snapshots of the walk the visitor is viewing, not canonical server-generated downloads. A GPS-derived copy may retain its chosen start and route only with a clear disclosure that sharing the file shares that information.

**Why:** The requested feature is a dependable, connection-free itinerary file without uploading or retaining visitor itineraries, locations or preferences. Email content has different semantics: it resolves published records on the server. Offline-file support does not authorise account storage, whole-site offline caching, fresh offline routing or email attachments.

**How to apply:** Keep future print/download changes local and snapshot-based. Do not reuse an email resolver that refetches or transmits the itinerary. Treat broader offline navigation, server retention and attachments as separately requested work.
